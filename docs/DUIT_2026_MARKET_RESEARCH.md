# DUIT 2026 — competitor evidence and pilot direction

Research date: **18 September 2026**. Primary official product, pricing and help pages only. These are vendor descriptions, not independently tested performance claims. Prices are USD as displayed, before tax, subject to plan/billing terms. No accounts were created and no paid services were used.

## Decision that matters

**A digital business card with AI notes is already a competitive category.** DUIT should compete on the quality of a complete job: introduce my business, remember the actual encounter, find a useful reason to reconnect, and turn that into an agreed next step. The strongest initial hypothesis is a personal business network that works across events and between events, with explicit **“I offer / I need”** information and a private memory of each encounter. This is a positioning hypothesis to validate, not a claim that competitors lack every component.

Suggested promise: **“Make the next meeting count.”** Supporting explanation: **“Share what you do. Remember who you met. Know what to do next.”** These are original proposed DUIT copy, not established market facts.

## The initial customer and use case

**Recommendation, to test:** founders and business-development staff at small B2B service/product businesses who attend recurring meetups or trade events and currently keep contacts in WhatsApp, phone contacts, cards and memory. Recruit 20–30 people from one accessible community, with a mix of buyers, vendors and partners. Start with one city/community and two real events; do not begin by trying to replace LinkedIn for everyone.

The user’s own situation is an excellent pilot script: “I met 20 people in Paris. Which three can help my business, which nine asked for my product, and what did I promise each of them?” The software must distinguish **people**, **encounters**, **exchange actions**, **reminders** and **opportunities**. One person can have several meetings and be both a buyer and supplier. A shared card does not prove that a meeting occurred or that a sale is likely.

A practical first journey:

1. A founder creates a useful business profile from a card and a short description. They review extracted details, a clear pitch and one primary CTA.
2. They share a link through WhatsApp or a QR code. The recipient opens an immediate, attractive web profile and can save details, ask about an offer or request a next step.
3. After the conversation, the sender captures a short private note: context, need, offer, promise and date. An event/location is chosen or confirmed.
4. DUIT suggests a small number of relevant contacts, showing the actual evidence: “You noted that Maya needs packaging; Arjun offers small-batch packaging. Met at the Delhi meetup.”
5. The sender reviews and sends a contextual follow-up using their preferred channel. They later record the result: reply, meeting booked, introduction made, opportunity opened or completed.

## Product choices that follow from the evidence

- **One stable business identity; many private encounters.** Public card information and private relationship notes must have separate ownership and visibility. Admin user management must not silently mean access to all private notes.
- **Useful receiving before sign-up.** Fast HTML card, readable services/products, real CTA, downloadable vCard and accessible contact controls. An install prompt is secondary. Sender-filled recipient details are a private draft, not a public profile or a verified identity.
- **AI inside tasks.** Scan → review; brief → editable pitch; note → confirmed next steps; question → contacts with cited evidence. Preserve original uploads and extracted source text. Show unknown fields rather than inventing a job title, phone number, credential or testimonial.
- **Explain recommendations in ordinary words.** Name the matching need/offer and the supporting note/profile section. Let the user mark useful/not useful. Avoid precise-looking “94% match” scores without calibrated meaning.
- **Make reviewing the default.** Drafting a message is not sending it. A WhatsApp share intent is not delivery or a response. Track those states truthfully.
- **Event mode must survive poor reception.** Cache the user’s card and recent contacts, queue notes and edits, show sync status, and prevent duplicate submissions. Distinguish cached QR/vCard sharing from an internet web page opening offline.
- **Use opt-in location sensibly.** Save the event/venue/city and source; allow manual correction. A card exchange should not require background location tracking. A map should answer a question, with a list alternative.
- **A calm daily workspace.** Home should show today’s useful next steps, recent people and a clear share/scan action. A network feed full of manufactured activity would distract from the actual task.
- **Measure useful outcomes.** Admin charts should separate profile views, saved cards, reciprocated exchanges, confirmed meetings, reviewed recommendations and user-reported business outcomes. Demo activity must be clearly separated from live pilot metrics.

These are design recommendations, not observed competitor limitations. The candidate advantage is their execution as one coherent loop for a specific audience.

## Pilot measures and decision thresholds

The following are **proposed targets**, not industry benchmarks or promises. Measure on consenting invited testers, distinguish seeded data, and report small-sample limits.

| Question | Instrumentation / initial target |
|---|---|
| Can people reach value quickly? | At least 70% of invited starters publish a reviewed profile and complete one real share in their first session; record median time and abandonment step. |
| Does receiving work without the app? | Card visible and primary action usable on Android/iOS browsers without login; target p75 LCP below 2.5s on the agreed mobile test profile. Track view → save/respond separately from install. |
| Does the app preserve meeting context? | At least 60% of logged pilot encounters have one useful note plus a confirmed next step or explicit “nothing needed.” |
| Is AI extraction trustworthy? | Test a labelled set of at least 50 varied cards. Report field-level exact-match accuracy, missing fields and invented fields separately. Target zero invented contact details; never claim perfect OCR from a small sample. |
| Are matches useful? | Collect relevance feedback on at least 100 suggestions. Initial target: at least 60% rated useful, with evidence shown and dismiss reasons captured. |
| Does follow-up actually happen? | Compare the proportion of intended follow-ups completed within 48 hours with each tester’s baseline. Aim for a measurable improvement; do not count opening WhatsApp as sending. |
| Does value survive the first event? | At least 40% of activated testers return to complete a meaningful action in week four. Track retrieving a contact, logging an encounter or completing a follow-up, not just app opens. |
| Will someone pay? | Conduct 10 pricing interviews after real usage and obtain at least three explicit paid-pilot commitments before declaring willingness to pay validated. |

## Pricing hypotheses to test

Competitors offer substantial free card functionality, so charging purely for a shareable card is a weak starting hypothesis. Blinq/HiHello demonstrate low single-digit annual team seat pricing, while personal relationship products charge for deeper ongoing utility; these are anchors, not proof of DUIT demand. [Blinq pricing](https://blinq.me/pricing), [HiHello pricing](https://www.hihello.com/pricing), [Dex pricing](https://getdex.com/pricing/)

- **Free:** one useful business profile, unlimited ordinary link/QR sharing, receiving/saving contacts, manual notes and a small AI trial allowance.
- **Individual Plus test:** ₹499–₹999/month for regular Indian users, or a separate $8–$15/month international test, with clearly bounded AI usage. These are proposed price experiments, not currency conversions or launch commitments.
- **Small team test:** ₹799–₹1,499 per active member/month, adding shared event workspaces, explicitly shared contacts/opportunities, admin controls and reporting. Test a minimum team package only after evidence that the buyer values it.
- **Do not launch complex billing yet.** Track real per-task inference/media cost, cap expensive image generation, and interview users after they experience value. Price the recurring workflow and useful outcome rather than implying all AI usage can be unlimited.

## What this research does not establish

This desk research does not establish market size, customer willingness to switch, retention, paid demand, legal compliance or a path to a particular company valuation. Vendor customer counts and claimed ROI were deliberately not used to forecast DUIT revenue. The next evidence should come from observed task completion and real pilot behaviour. The private archive is useful for design variety and restoration history; it is not a current active-user base or permission to contact former users.
