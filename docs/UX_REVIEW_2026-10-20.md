# Personal DUIT UX review — 20 October 2026

## Review evidence and limits

Reviewed the rendered React Native web app in Chrome at a 412px-wide local preview, rebuilt from main d477811. Used Maya's populated local workspace and the local new-user review account. No contacts, meetings, enquiries or messages were submitted. No application code changed. This is a browser UX review, not a native Android acceptance test; camera OCR results, phone contact integration, physical GPS, WhatsApp delivery, offline recovery and AI output quality were not tested.

## Features observed in the rendered app

- Cards: visual person-first feed with visiting-card artwork and business-photo collage; whole row opens a carousel; search and filter controls.
- Add: scan business card, choose gallery photo, scan DUIT QR, start with a name, paste DUIT link.
- Manual entry: name, role, company, email, phone, date/time, place, GPS button, city/country, event suggestions, text/voice notes, tidy-note action, meeting type and next step. Submit label is “Save this meeting”.
- Share: recipient name, country code, WhatsApp number, discussion topic/details, potential-lead toggle, timestamp, location/event and editable message; disabled action explains missing recipient name.
- Sent: recipient history with phone, date/time, place and notes; opens meeting context.
- Meetings: timeline, events and places; date shortcuts; encounter histories and notes.
- Received detail: portrait/card/business carousel, fixed CTA, about/contact details, meetings, next steps and follow-up draft action. CTA opens enquiry form with interest choices, timing and contact fields.
- My Card: carousel, share, edit, activity, another card, incoming messages and company dashboard.
- Editor: personal details, photo/card upload, photo polish/card cleanup actions, website, business description, AI improvement, photos/video, visual generation, action label, and hook/relevance/offer/outcome/proof/CTA fields.
- Company dashboard: people, live cards, enquiries, company settings and teammate invitations.
- New-user home: example cards and create-card button. Creating a card from home first navigates to My Card, requiring another Create my card tap. Create another card is visible before a first card exists.

## Concrete UX issues

1. Adding a simple contact becomes a multi-section meeting form; the save label describes the wrong primary job.
2. Manual contact phone field asks for a country code in one input while Share has a separate country picker: inconsistent capture.
3. Cards, Sent and Meetings distribute one person's information across three destinations.
4. Large feed artwork allows only roughly one full person and the start of another in the reviewed viewport. Attractive for discovery, slow for finding an existing contact.
5. The received-card screen prioritizes a business enquiry over direct personal contact actions; no Save to phone control was found in the inspected capture/detail/settings flows (native behavior not verified).
6. My Card prominently offers Enquire on the owner's own card and places Messages/My business before it.
7. The editor exposes product-writing concepts such as hook, relevance, outcome and proof as raw fields.
8. The X/Close action in the multi-step editor returns to the previous step rather than closing immediately. A back arrow would communicate that better.
9. Example profiles appear under Your cards; new-user filter says Show 0 cards. This blurs examples versus actually saved people.
10. The sharing form places the final action after several context sections; immediate sharing should not require working through optional detail.

## Proposed direction — not implementation decisions

- Main destinations: People, Share, My Card. Add person is a prominent action on People. Keep Sent as a People filter/history and meetings inside each person.
- People can be saved without being DUIT users and without having a polished public business profile. A scanned card remains a private contact record unless its owner separately publishes a card.
- Capture: scan or enter details, review extracted fields, save. Preserve the original image. Put uncertain OCR fields in front of the user. Avoid invented details.
- Offer optional Save to phone after saving to DUIT. Start with one-way create/update linked to the device contact, with duplicate detection; full two-way sync remains an open decision.
- Share: offer QR/link immediately; WhatsApp needs recipient phone and optionally name. Context is auto-suggested, editable and optional. Do not equate opening WhatsApp with successful delivery.
- After saving a person, offer Share my card with them. Link repeated meetings/shares to the same person instead of creating duplicates.
- Keep time, place and short notes as compact context; distinguish the time a card was scanned from the actual meeting date. Saving must still work when location is denied/unavailable.
- Offline contact access, pending-save status, retry without duplicates, export and recovery are reliability requirements to validate, not newly verified features.
- Hide team administration, lead pipelines and elaborate pitch editing from the personal primary flow. Preserve existing implementation until agreed changes.

## Questions for the next discussion

1. Is phone sync a per-contact Save to phone action or automatic two-way sync? Recommended first step: per-contact one-way save/update, with a remembered preference later.
2. Should sharing always save the recipient in People? Recommended: yes when contact details exist, with an explicit shared-card event and no claim of confirmed WhatsApp delivery.
3. Should time/place be captured automatically but remain optional, retaining the earlier meeting-memory direction? Recommended: yes; never block contact saving.
4. Should People default to compact person rows with a card thumbnail, opening rich visual carousels on tap? Recommended: yes for repeated personal retrieval.
