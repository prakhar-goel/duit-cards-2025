1) Think that when you share your business card with someone. What is your first thought? That this person would be contacting me whenever they need my services. For this 2 things are important. 1) they should be able to recollect me quickly even several weeks/months/years from now. 2) I should have given them my elevator pitch of my business/product/service and a compelling reason to reach out to me, or maybe they need me now, and they are simply able to give me their lead right from my business card (foot in the door). This is the experience we are trying to build. 

And vice versa - when I receive a card, instead of putting inside my drawer, I want to save them in such a way that I am able to find them back, when I need their product/service or when I think I have a product or service for them. 

For this, we use AI to create not just a business card, but also an elevator pitch of the person. Its like a bespoke presentation of 5-6 slides/a small video etc of extremely important points shared in a very condusive way, quickly browsable. Like a hook, with appropriate CTA. Think of it like veritasium videos on youtube. His videos create an interest in the 5 secs of watching. It is because the videos have strong initial hooks and are very interesting.
